# ECPPM 2026 Conference Paper - Heat Pump Information Specifications

Supplementary material for the paper *Critical Information Requirements for
Building Service Product Selection: An Evaluation of Open Information
Specification Formats* presented at ECPPM 2026.

This repository contains example IDS and LOIN specifications used to evaluate
how critical heat-pump product selection requirements can be represented in
open, machine-interpretable formats.

## Companion study

The requirements evaluated here were identified and ranked in the companion
CIB W78 2026 paper *Mixed-Methods Evaluation of Critical Data Requirements for
Heat Pump Product Configuration*. Its survey data, codebook, figures and
analysis tables are available at:

<https://github.com/Design-Computation-RWTH/2026-cib-w78-heat-pump-properties>
